Thanks & Credits

Cze & Peku

The maps and location art throughout these adventures come from Cze & Peku — a studio producing stunning battle maps and scene illustrations for tabletop games. If you're running any kind of TTRPG campaign, their work is worth every look.

Dungeon Influence

This adventure wouldn't exist without Dungeon Influence. They regularly put out amazing adventure hooks, NPC ideas, and more — and one of them wouldn't leave my head: "Several fighter and monster statues have disappeared across the continent. A mysterious organisation is creating an army." The Statue Heist grew out of that seed. Huge thanks for the spark.
