## Introduction - Player Pitch

Few months ago whilst mapping an island now referred to as  **SoftEarth** a discovery was made - magically pliable & soft marble-like substance on the island. Substance so amazingly magical that it surrenders to the intent of whoever is handling the brown marble-like-material. Making it possible it theoretically shape it into anything. **The Horizon Keepers** closely investigated the material & deemed it completely safe - ready to be released for public use.

This discovery sparked a great interest in the Artisinal community of **The Drifting Crown** Now not only stoneworkers could shape stone & marble into beautiful statues & building - but tailors, bakers - all sorts of artists & artisans could give life to their vision using this amazing material & after they finished their work, within a few days of undisturbed 'Drying' . it would turn to stone.

And now a contest is being held is being held in the **Magister's Market**! The artisinal Heart of the **Drifting Crown**
Shape the statue to be displayed in the **Horizon Keeper's**** Library Courtyard! A chance for any artist to display their work for centuries to come in the prestigious adventurer's guild!
And on this beautiful morning atop the drifting crown - several of the most promising statues - have disappeared! 

A bounty is put out, horizon keepers are called to investigate -  and that is where our adventure starts

Is your character a horizon keeper? - A participant in the contest? - whoever they are, you must design your character so that they are eager to find out what happened and work with the other adventurers.

**5 Room Structure:**

**1. Investigation — The Market Plaza** Statues and Tarin are missing. Players are called in on Day 2. They investigate the plaza — stone fragments near buildings, a trail leading out that soon disappears. NPCs point in different directions. If players can't pick up the trail, **Crok Ruthesk** can help with a magic item in exchange for a favor.

**2. Puzzle / Exploration — The Museum** Clues and NPC testimony lead to the museum. Players can sneak in or confront **Gorrath** directly. **Melandra** is present — visibly ill, warmly dismissed by **Gorrath**.

Inside: a covered wing, missing magic items including the **Ring of Gravity** now on Gorrath's hand.

When confronted, **Gorrath's default is to lie.** He expresses concern, plays along, and at the right moment "deduces" that one of the ruins he surveyed did seem unusual — almost like a cracked prison. He offers to guide them there himself. His real goal is to take the long route and buy **Tarin** more time.

At DM's Discretion: **Automatic Insight checks** for the highest roller — they can tell something is off.

From here the session branches:

- **Players expose him** → fight breaks out (resource-draining, possible chase scene) or he runs → race to the dungeon → **debuffed dragon**
- **Gorrath successfully deceives them** → he travels with them → **betrayal on the way or at the dungeon** → **Buffed dragon**

If he runs rather than fights, he ends up alongside **Kaldorzen** in the final confrontation. As long as **Melandra** lives, he doesn't care if she hates him for what he's had to do to save her.

**3. Setback — Getting There & The Dungeon Entrance** Players take **Pete's airship** to the island. The dungeon entrance seal is already broken — a way in exists. Throwing something of value into the everburning brazier summons **Merandiel Frostborn**, who shares what he knows and points them to Magical Items left in the first chamber. The Frescas show Kaldorzen in many forms — all with red glowing eyes.

**4. Climax — The Dungeon** The stone army waits in the second chamber — sealed in magical ice. The boss chamber: **Tarin** mid-sculpt, stone creatures awakening, Gorrath's betrayal. Players must stop the army from being completed. To seal Kaldorzen back they must bring her to half health and then recite the oath **uninterrupted for 3 rounds** within 60ft of her. 

**5. Resolution**
- **Success** → Kaldorzen sealed, Tarin rescued, army never walks
- **Partial** → Dragon awakens weakened
- **Failure** → **Kaldorzen** escapes — a problem for another day

## Pete & The Airship Dock

### Overview

Players need transport to SoftEarth. Pete runs the ship that goes to Softearth, transporting explorers who map the island, archeologists - anyone on official business, including **Horizon Keepers**  he's not hard to convince, but he _will_ ask for a magic item in exchange. He's a terrible haggler; if players push back even slightly, or mention they are on **Horizon Keeper** Business, he folds. If asked, he remembers taking **Gorrath** out recently — _"Archaeological dig, he said. Brought a lot of cargo. Didn't ask questions."_

At DM's discretion, players automatically can guess that they would have to go to the **Airship Dock** for transport - or they can find out by asking around.
### Pete

Airship pilot. Chatty, Old Gnome, Messy hair, a little nosy, deeply unqualified-feeling for someone trusted with an airship. He asks for a magic item upfront with the confidence of a man who thinks this is a reasonable request — and immediately starts second-guessing himself the moment anyone hesitates.

- **Haggling:** He opens high. Any pushback — a raised eyebrow, a sigh, _"really?"_ — and he starts offering to take less. He'll settle for coin, a promise, or a good story.
- **On Gorrath:** He remembers the trip clearly. _"Oh, Gorrath? Yeah — yesterday, early. Big load of cargo, wouldn't say what. Archaeological dig, he told me. Seemed in a hurry."_ He can point them to the exact landing spot on SoftEarth.
- **During the flight:** Pete talks. A lot. DM's discretion how useful or distracting that is.

### Second Chamber
#### Second Chamber Overview
An enormous frozen crypt. Hundreds of earth elemental warriors stand encased in magical ice — Kaldorzen's petrified army. The chamber is utterly still and cold. This serves as a visual reminder of the threat: if sealing fails, this army walks. The ice itself is unbreakable by normal means and reinforced by the prison's magic. Players cannot prevent the fight ahead by trying to destroy the army here.

>[!readaloud] 
>This chamber is completely dark& extremely cold - -other than the faintest source of light coming the very other end & exit of the room- anyone has source of light?
>
_This gigantic hall stretches further than your torchlight can reach — tens of feet tall, filled with row upon row of pillar-like pedestals. Atop each one stands a figure. Their arms, their torso — they are not humanoid. They wear no armor. You soon realize what you are looking at: earth elementals. Primordial warriors. Hundreds of them — enough to threaten cities. Kingdoms._
>
_But they are not moving._
>
_Each one is encased in ice — thick, pale blue, perfectly smooth. The cold radiates off them in waves. Whatever imprisoned Kaldorzen imprisoned her army alongside her, sealed in the same moment, frozen mid-stance. Some still have their fists raised. Some are mid-step. The ice has held for centuries._
>
It is still holding. For now.
>
The Ice is immune to all low level magic-damage. it is instantly evident that whatever magic create it is powerful & beyond being melted or damaged via conventional magical ways.

### Boss Chamber
#### Boss Chamber Overview
The final confrontation. Kaldorzen stands in serpent form channeling power into a nearly-completed dragon sculpture crafted by Tarin. Two awakened stone guardians (Owlbear and Minotaur) defend her. Players must damage Kaldorzen to half health, then protect one reader reciting the sealing oath for three uninterrupted rounds within 60ft. The dragon's lair action becomes the primary threat during sealing — she uses it to wall off the reader. Success seals her - failure means she escapes.

### Sealing Mechanic 

**Trigger:** Once Kaldorzen hits half health the dragon staggers — a visible crack appears across her chest and golden light bleeds through. The scroll activates, begins glowing. Players know this is the moment.

**The mechanic:**

- One player reads (both hands occupied, cannot move, cannot attack, cannot cast)
- Breaks if: they move or are forcibly moved
- Round counter resets on interruption — doesn't just pause
- Each successful round: the crack on the dragon widens, light gets brighter — visual feedback showing it's working

**Why the dragon's legendary action matters here:** Stone pillars and barriers become specifically threatening now — she tries to wall off the reader from the rest of the party. The fight shifts from "kill the dragon" to "protect the reader while finishing the dragon off." That's a clean mechanical pivot that makes the legendary action feel purposeful.

**Round 3 payoff:** The crack shatters open, **Kaldorzen** is pulled screaming back into the prison, the dragon crumbles.

## Ending

### Sucess - Kaldorzen is Sealed
> [!readaloud] _The crack tears open._
_You feel a deep vibration through the soles of your feet, through your bones. The golden light explodes outward from the cracks of the dragon's chest, flooding the chamber in a blinding wash, and Kaldorzen's voice cuts off as her essence is Pulled down and inward into the tomb at the end of this chamber, like water rushing into a drain, until there is nothing left of her._
>
_The dragon goes still and it begins to come apart  -  crumbling, piece by piece, the carved scales sliding off in slow quiet falls. The torches die one by one, until only the pale column of light from the ceiling crack remains._

### Failure — Kaldorzen Escapes

If players fail to seal her in time, Kaldorzen breaks free — but she's still not done. To truly be free she needs Merandiel defeated, and her current army isn't enough for that.

She puts **Tarin** back to work. She will spend the coming days/weeks animating a slightly larger force — pulling stone from the island, from ruins, from whatever she can reach. Once the army is ready, **Gorrath** sends it against Merandiel's. If she wins that fight, nothing stops her.

### Aftermath 

**Tarin** is physically unharmed but shaken. He doesn't say much. He'll need a moment before he's ready to move. If players approach him gently, he'll eventually talk. Let the players lead.

**The ice army** remains. Kaldorzen's sealing re-anchors the prison's magic — the ice holds. The army isn't going anywhere. Players may ask what happens to them; the honest answer is: someone will have to deal with that eventually. Not today.

**Gorrath — three branches depending on how the session went:**

- _He was captured at the museum:_ He learns from the players that it's over. The pact is void. The cure never came. However players tell him — gently or not — it lands hard. He cooperates fully and doesn't resist. Whether he faces consequences is a player decision.
- _He fled and fought alongside Kaldorzen:_ When she's pulled back into the prison, his reason to fight vanishes. He stops. Falls to his knees filled with despair - unable to save the only thing in this world that means most to him - everything to him. What happens next is up to the players.

**Melandra's fate — DM's call:**

> This is still an open question. Two options:
> 
> - **Darker ending:** The pact is void. Melandra's illness continues. Gorrath got nothing. This is the tragic read — his betrayal cost everyone and bought him nothing.
> - **Leave it open:** The players now know about **Melandra**. They could seek another way — a lead on the medusa, a healer, something. Gorrath has a reason to cooperate rather than simply collapse. Recommended if you want the door open for a follow-up or want players to feel agency beyond the fight.
